# Holiday Beach Bag Gift Guide | Coastal Picks for Every Budget

The holidays are coming and if you are shopping for someone who would rather be at the beach than anywhere else, I have you covered.

This is a living gift guide that I will keep updating as I test more bags, towels, and accessories. For now, here are my top picks at every price point — all chosen by someone who actually drags these things across Florida sand on a regular basis.

## Under $50

**The Turkish Towel.** Lighter than a traditional beach towel, dries in minutes, and rolls up to nothing. Mine has survived a full Florida summer and still looks brand new. Perfect for the person who complains about bulky beach bags.

**The Mesh Tote.** Sand falls out. Water drains. You can see your keys without digging. Under $20 and genuinely life-changing for beach days.

## Under $100

**The Woven Straw Bag.** Big enough for a towel, sunscreen, a book, and snacks. Leather handles that do not dig into your shoulder. Looks just as good at a farmers market as it does on the sand.
